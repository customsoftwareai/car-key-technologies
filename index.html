What We Do

Full-spectrum digital key & fob engineering.

From transponder duplication to phone-as-key credentialing, our technicians and partner network handle every layer of automotive access and immobilizer technology.

Transponder Key Programming

Cutting, cloning, and programming for chipped ignition keys across virtually every OEM transponder protocol.

Smart Key & PEPS Systems

Proximity and passive entry / passive start key programming, rolling code sync, and fob battery service.

Immobilizer & Security Gateways

PATS, VATS, SKIM and IMMO programming, OEM security-gateway compliant access, and ECU/BCM key relearn.

All-Keys-Lost Recovery

OBD-II and bench-based all-keys-lost recovery with VIN-based code retrieval and on-site emergency response.

Fleet Key Management

Bulk provisioning, asset-linked credentialing, and master-key programs for fleets, rental depots, and auctions.

Digital Key & Mobile Credentialing

Phone-as-key setup support across BLE, NFC, and UWB platforms for the latest connected vehicles.

How It Works

From diagnosis to a working key, coordinated end to end.

Diagnose

We identify the vehicle's key system, transponder type, and security architecture from VIN and module data.

Dispatch

A vetted partner technician is matched and routed based on location, vehicle platform, and job complexity.

Program & Verify

Keys are cut, programmed, and verified against OEM security protocols on site or at the point of service.

Confirm & Support

Documentation is logged and support stays available around the clock for fleets, dealers, and drivers.
