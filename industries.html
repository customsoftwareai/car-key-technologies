Who We Serve

Purpose-built key solutions for every part of the automotive ecosystem.

Every segment of the industry has different pressure points around key turnaround. We tailor programming, dispatch, and volume support to match.

Automotive Dealerships

New and certified pre-owned lots need spare and replacement keys turned around fast, without disrupting the sales floor. We support duplicate key production at intake, all-keys-lost recovery on trade-ins, and smart key programming for delivery-ready vehicles.

Trade-in key recoveryDelivery-ready programmingWholesale key supply

Fleet Management Companies

Multi-vehicle operators need consistency across makes and models, plus a way to track who holds which key. We provide bulk provisioning, asset-linked credentialing, and depot-level master key programs sized to fleet volume.

Bulk provisioningTelematics-aligned credentialingDepot master keying

Rental Car Agencies

High vehicle turnover means keys go missing more often, and every day a car sits idle costs revenue. Our on-site response and spare-key programs keep rental fleets moving between rentals.

Rapid on-site responseSpare key programsReturn-lane support

Auto Auctions & Remarketers

Auction lanes run on tight timelines. We support high-volume key replacement and duplication ahead of sale day so vehicles cross the block with working, verified keys.

Pre-sale key prepHigh-volume duplicationTime-sensitive dispatch

Insurance & Collision Repair

When a claim involves a lost, stolen, or damaged key, we coordinate replacement on a timeline that fits the broader repair or claims process, reducing vehicle downtime and rental days.

Claims-aligned schedulingCollision-center coordination

Independent Repair Shops & Locksmiths

We work behind the scenes as a technical resource and wholesale supplier for independent shops and locksmiths who need OEM-grade key data, cutting, or programming support beyond their in-house capability.

Wholesale key supplyTechnical backup support

Consumers & Drivers

Locked out, lost a fob, or need a spare cut? Our strategic partner network puts a certified technician within reach across the United States and Canada, day or night.

24/7 emergency dispatchOn-site service
